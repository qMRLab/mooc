Myelin water fraction and echo time

Using Eq. 3.8 and the MW/IEW T2 values estimated in Exercise 2, calculate the myelin water fraction (MWF) values at the following TEs: 25, 50, 75, 100, 200, 300. Assume that there is 50% MW and 50% IEW.

$$\textit{MWF} = \frac{S_{MW}e^{-TE/T_{2,MW}}}{S_{MW}e^{-TE/T_{2,MW}} + S_{IEW}e^{-TE/T_{2,IEW}}}$$

The T2 values of Exercise 2c.

  • The voxel is 50% MW at every TE, yet Eq. 3.8 drops: it is MW’s share of the signal at that TE, and MW decays 6× faster.
  • Only TE → 0 gives the true 50%: MWF fits extrapolate to TE = 0, so they need short echoes.
Type the class's T2 values from Exercise 2c if they differ. Three clicks. 1: Eq. 3.8 at the six TEs, in the table and as points: about 25% at 25 ms, 10% at 50 ms, 3.5% at 75 ms, 1.2% at 100 ms, and practically zero at 200 and 300 ms. 2: the whole curve: it starts at the true 50% at TE = 0 and falls fast. 3: what it means: Eq. 3.8 with a TE is the fraction of the measured signal that comes from MW at that echo, not the fraction of water in the voxel. That is only the same at TE = 0. This is why MWF imaging fits all echoes with a multi-exponential model and reads the fractions at TE = 0, and why its protocols start with a short first echo, around 10 ms, and space the echoes closely.
