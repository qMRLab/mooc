Two interactive exercises on echoes and the CPMG echo train. **Exercise 2-1** is a refresher on echoes: what a pulse's phase does, spin echoes and stimulated echoes, and what an imperfect 180° does. **Exercise 2-2** measures _T_{sub}`2` with an echo train: a timing game with perfect pulses, then with B{sub}`1` errors, the CPMG condition, and which echoes to fit. Pick an exercise to open its slides. Use **Open in new tab ↗** (top right of the slides) to present them in their own window. Click the slides first; the arrow keys play the games, so use the grey bars at the left and right edges (or Space and Page Down) to move between slides, **F** for full screen, **S** for speaker notes and **Esc** for the overview.
