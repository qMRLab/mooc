Three interactive exercises on echoes and the CPMG echo train. **Exercise 2-1** is on RF phase and spin echoes: what the ±x and ±y RF phases do, spin echoes with perfect and imperfect pulses, and single-point measurements of _T_{sub}`2` and _T_{sub}`2`{sup}`*`. **Exercise 2-2** is on stimulated echoes: three pulses, what an imperfect 180° does, and why every echo of a train mixes spin and stimulated echoes. **Exercise 2-3** measures _T_{sub}`2` with an echo train: a timing game with perfect pulses, then with B{sub}`1` errors, the CPMG condition, and which echoes to fit. Pick an exercise to open its slides. Use **Open in new tab ↗** (top right of the slides) to present them in their own window. Click the slides first; the arrow keys play the games, so use the grey bars at the left and right edges (or Space and Page Down) to move between slides, **F** for full screen, **S** for speaker notes and **Esc** for the overview.
