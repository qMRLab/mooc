Interactive exercises on the double angle method and AFI. **Exercise 4-1** builds intuition: can you guesstimate B{sub}`1` from the raw data? two images of a brain at 60° and 120°, a quiz on where B{sub}`1` is high or low, and why the double angle works. **Exercise 4-2** is on registering qMRI measurements between each other: a subject moved between their two double angle scans; register them to match an AFI B{sub}`1` map (a refresher from Lab 4). **Exercise 4-3** builds intuition for AFI: one magnetization vector through the AFI cycle, its two readouts locked in, to see why the two signals depend on B{sub}`1`. Pick an exercise to open its slides. Use **Open in new tab ↗** (top right of the slides) to present them in their own window. Click the slides first; some slides use the arrow keys, so use the grey bars at the left and right edges (or Space and Page Down) to move between slides, **F** for full screen and **Esc** for the overview.
