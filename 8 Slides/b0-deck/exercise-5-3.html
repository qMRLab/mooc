Exercise 5-3 · Summary

Summary

  1. B0 maps let us compute the shim currents that reduce the field inhomogeneity.
  2. Volume-wise shimming minimizes the mean and the SD of Δf over the whole masked volume; slice-wise shimming does it over a single slice only.
  3. Slice-wise shimming can give better shims for multislice acquisitions, with the shims updated for each slice.
