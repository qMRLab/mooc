Exercise 5-1 · Summary

Summary

  1. Two echoes give ΔB0 from the change in phase between them: ΔB0 = Δφ / (γ ΔTE), with Δφ in radians and ΔTE in seconds.
  2. Fat precesses 3.4 ppm slower than water (−434 Hz at 3 T), so in a voxel with fat the measured phase is that of the sum of the two, and the B0 measured is biased.
  3. Echoes taken when water and fat are in phase (every 2.30 ms at 3 T) remove that bias.
