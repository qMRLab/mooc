Can you tell fast from slow?
  • Rates from 0.5× to 1.5× move the hand between 0.5 Δt and 1.5 Δt in Δt: a spread of Δt minutes.
  • One fit only while that spread is under one turn: Δt < 60 min.
  • In MRI: offsets from −Δfmax to +Δfmax spread the phase by 2Δfmax·ΔTE turns, so ΔTE < 1/(2Δfmax).

Exercise 5-1 · Summary

A phase image is a clock you only glance at

  1. Each echo is one glance: the phase shows where the hand points, not how many turns it made since the last echo.
  2. Knowing the range is what makes two glances enough: one rate fits as long as the whole range of offsets moves the phase by less than one turn, ΔTE < 1/(2Δfmax).
  3. Past that, the map aliases: a too-large offset reads as a smaller one of the other sign. That’s a phase wrap in time. So how would you measure an offset larger than the limit?

    Glance more often (a shorter ΔTE), or add a third echo at a different spacing (the multi-echo methods and UMPIRE of the lecture).
